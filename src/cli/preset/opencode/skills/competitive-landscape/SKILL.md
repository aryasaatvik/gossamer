# Competitive Landscape

Use PageGraph's public-page corpus and product context to decide which domains compete for the same
need. Read [Executor Starters](references/executor.md) before collecting live market evidence.

Sample representative queries, find domains that recur, and compare their page types, intent,
coverage, differentiation, and authority signals. Separate direct product competitors, publishers,
directories, communities, and incidental results. Study only the leaders whose evidence could
change a decision.

Return the structured state requested by the workflow. Do not copy competitor claims, treat every
ranking keyword as desirable, or update a provider's saved competitor list.
