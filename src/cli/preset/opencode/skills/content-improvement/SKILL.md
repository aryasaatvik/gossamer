# Content Improvement

Research the target page exactly as described by [Executor Starters](references/executor.md). During
the research turn, diagnose intent, substance, differentiation, support, and sibling-page ownership
without editing files.

During the host-authorized action turn, apply only recommendations accepted by the decision report.
Preserve the repository's public positioning and voice, edit the owning source rather than generated
output, and keep the change local to the selected page and intent. Do not invent metrics, customers,
capabilities, or citations.

Leave the resulting repository diff uncommitted for review. Provider state remains read-only.
