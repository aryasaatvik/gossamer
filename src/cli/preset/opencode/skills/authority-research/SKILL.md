# Authority Research

Use PageGraph's pages to identify the asset that could reasonably earn each citation. Read
[Executor Starters](references/executor.md) before collecting backlink or live-page evidence.

Use backlink gaps only to nominate sources. Verify the current page and qualify topical relevance,
legitimacy, editorial fit, audience overlap, and eligibility. Reject paid-link schemes, bulk
directories, expired pages, scraped lists, and opportunities with no suitable destination asset.

Return the structured state requested by the workflow. Contact discovery, outreach, submission,
and provider project updates are separate operations and remain outside this research workflow.
